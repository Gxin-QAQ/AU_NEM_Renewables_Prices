INTERPRETATION BOUNDARY

These are conditional associations, not causal effects.

Realised renewable output, demand, outages, network constraints, bidding and prices are jointly determined. The project’s identification audit rejected a simple weather-IV causal claim; this dashboard preserves that boundary rather than overstating the evidence.
